Clinical Quality Measures Engine

HEDIS / CMS eCQM · Synthea synthetic non-PHI EHR

A declarative measure engine over a 13,810-patient EHR — 820,993 encounters and 10.8M observations. Measures are data, not code: each is a YAML spec with its SNOMED CT and LOINC value sets held separately, so an annual steward update is a reviewable diff a clinical analyst can sign off rather than a code change with regression risk.

CMS122 reports 17.3% of diabetic patients in poor glycaemic control. Decomposing that numerator: 203 of the 204 were counted because they were never tested at all. It is a testing-outreach problem, not a glycaemic-control problem — and no amount of clinical intervention on control would move the number.

Limitation. Synthea produces realistic structure but not realistic clinical behaviour. Breast Cancer Screening at 5.5% is a generator artifact, not a finding about care. The engine is tested; the rates describe no real population.

Claims Denials & Appeals

CMS Transparency in Coverage, 2021–2023 · real public filings

Three years of every Qualified Health Plan on the federal Marketplace — 15,545 plan-years, 624 issuer-years, 126.9 million denied claims. Built as a SQL panel using window functions over a two-grain fact design.

Roughly one claim in six is denied. Only 0.168% are ever formally appealed — and 40.1% of appeals are overturned. The rate holds across all three years, so the bottleneck is not claim merit. It is capacity to file.

Limitation. The data is aggregate and self-reported by issuers. No claim-level detail, no denial reason codes at claim level, and no way to tell whether successful appeals differ systematically from unsuccessful ones. It sizes the gap; it cannot work a single claim.

Emergency Department Throughput

CDC/NCHS NHAMCS 2022 · real national survey microdata

16,025 real ED visits, survey-weighted to 155,397,747 national visits with ultimate-cluster variance. The pipeline refuses to run unless it reproduces CDC's published national total — get the weighting wrong on a complex survey and you still get a number, just a wrong one, and nothing tells you.

It is not when, it is where. Between-hospital variation in door-to-provider time is 3.56× larger than between-hour variation. And the peak-hour effect everyone optimises for is not statistically detectable — difference 4.87 min, p = 0.406. Reported as a null result rather than dropped.

Limitation. A survey estimates, it does not census — every figure carries a confidence interval and an NCHS reliability flag. The variation decomposition covers only the 45 hospitals with 100+ sampled visits.

30-Day Readmission Risk

UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals · real de-identified encounters

99,343 real inpatient encounters. The model reaches AUC 0.664 — and the argument of the project is that AUC is the wrong thing to judge it on. No care-management team consumes a probability; they consume a ranked list they have capacity to work.

Working the top 20% of the list catches 37.7% of all readmissions, at 1.89× enrichment and a number-needed-to-screen of 4.8. Separately: the logistic model ranks nearly as well but its mean predicted risk is 46.3% against an observed 11.1% — good discrimination, unusable probabilities.

Two results that did not go my way, kept anyway. Patient-grouped splitting moved AUC by 0.0023 — less than the fold-to-fold standard deviation. And isotonic recalibration made calibration worse. Both stayed in the repository.

Experimentation Toolkit

simulation against known ground truth

Five ways online experiments quietly fail, each measured rather than asserted — because the only way to know whether an estimator recovers the truth is to run it on data where you set the truth.

Peeking ten times at a null experiment drives the false positive rate from a nominal 5% to 18.5%. And a Simpson's paradox where overall lift is −41.3% while both segments are positive — with the global SRM check passing at p = 1.00. Only the per-segment check catches it.

Scope. This is product-analytics methodology rather than healthcare. It is here because the statistics are the strongest in the set, not because it is on the path I am hiring into.
